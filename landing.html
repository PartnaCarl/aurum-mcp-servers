Under the hood

What makes AURUM different.

Built by an FBA seller, not a SaaS company. Every number is sourced, every threshold has a reason.

2026 fee tables, updated

The April 2026 3.5% FBA surcharge is baked into every fee tier. Amazon's in-house calculator shows pre-surcharge numbers for another six weeks. You'll know the real cost today.

LIVE fee modeling

Amazon's Low Inventory Value fee fires at 28 days of coverage or less. Most tools don't account for it. AURUM does — and surfaces it as a flag before you order.

Slow-mover surcharge

Inventory sitting past 182 days triggers Amazon's long-term storage surcharge. AURUM projects your days-to-sell at real velocity and warns you before you buy 12 months of product.

715 brands, not 50

The compliance database covers gated, IP-risky, and commingling-risk brands across 14 categories — electronics, apparel, sunglasses (Ray-Ban, Oakley), baby, supplements, grocery, automotive, pro audio.

Return rate adjustment

Apparel has a 25% return rate. Grocery has 3%. AURUM adjusts your net margin by category before scoring, so "20% margin" apparel doesn't trick you into thinking it's a winner.
